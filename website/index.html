Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before installing.

Does it block any websites?
No! The 7 WebLY Sins never blocks anything. It simply shows a gentle banner message to help you be more mindful about your browsing habits.
Is my browsing data private?
100%. Everything stays on your device. We never collect, store, or send your browsing data anywhere. Zero tracking, zero analytics, zero servers.
Is this a religious app?
The 7 deadly sins are a universal framework for self-improvement that resonates across cultures. While faith-friendly, anyone can benefit from building better digital habits.
Is it safe for kids?
Absolutely. There is no inappropriate content whatsoever. The messages are fun, positive, and educational — designed to build character, not shame.
How do Virtue Tokens work?
You earn 1 Virtue Token for every 5 minutes spent on productive sites like email, learning platforms, fitness apps, or job boards. Tokens are permanent and you can share your total on Facebook!
Can I turn off specific sins?
Yes! With Premium, you can go to the WebLY Sins tab in the extension popup and toggle off any sins you don't want to be warned about. Free users get all 7 sins monitored by default.
What browsers does it work on?
Currently available for Google Chrome and any Chromium-based browser (Edge, Brave, Opera, etc.).
